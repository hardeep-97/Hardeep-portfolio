# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is recruiters, hiring managers, and engineering leaders in India and globally who are evaluating Hardeep for software engineering, backend, full-stack, or DevOps roles. They need to understand his role fit, experience, technical breadth, delivery outcomes, and available proof quickly.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio helps Hardeep find his next engineering role by turning his Resume into a concise, credible, evidence-first web experience. Success means a qualified visitor can quickly verify his capabilities, inspect real work, view his Resume, and contact him about a relevant opportunity.

## Positioning

Hardeep is a Software and DevOps Developer who can own both application code and the path to reliable production delivery.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary and secondary CTA: Contact Hardeep about a role is primary; viewing his Resume and professional profiles is secondary.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: “I build reliable software and the systems that ship it.”
- Belief ladder: Hardeep is open to relevant roles; his experience matches the visitor’s needs; he has produced measurable delivery and stability improvements; his projects demonstrate real engineering ownership; contacting him is direct and low-friction.
- Proof on hand: the current Resume at `assets/resume/hardeep-shiyani-resume.pdf`; more than six years of professional experience; PHP, Laravel, Node.js, Python, AWS, Docker, GitLab CI/CD, and AI-assisted development experience; measurable delivery and quality improvements; the hTunnel open-source project; the Sweet Night Admin freelance project; and a published research paper listed in the Resume.

## Brand Personality

Practical, dependable, and confident. The voice is direct and professional without sounding corporate or inflated. Visitors should feel that Hardeep is technically capable, honest about his work, and ready to take ownership.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy or slow animation, generic claims without evidence, crowded skill-logo walls, hidden Resume or contact paths, positioning Hardeep only as a Tech Lead, prominent education content, personal photography, and template-like or obviously AI-generated visuals.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with employability: make role fit, availability, and contact options clear in the first screen.
2. Show proof before decoration: use measurable outcomes, real project screenshots, and direct project links.
3. Make recruiter scanning effortless: keep hierarchy obvious, copy concise, and technical breadth contextual.
4. Present breadth without narrowing Hardeep to one title or filling the page with buzzwords.
5. Keep every path to contact, Resume, and project proof direct.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The portfolio should meet WCAG AA expectations with semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, useful alternative text, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support. Content must remain understandable without animation, icon fonts, or a personal photograph.
